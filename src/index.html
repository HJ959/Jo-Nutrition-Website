Psychotherapist & Nutritional Therapist

I am Joanna Oates, a neuro-affirmative psychotherapist and nutritional therapist. I offer psychological support alongside practical nutrition and lifestyle guidance to:

  • Individuals navigating disordered eating, body image concerns, and eating disorders (working alongside your existing medical team).
  • Clients seeking general nutritional and lifestyle support alongside medically supervised weight management.
  • Parents and carers supporting loved ones with disordered eating and body image concerns.

As a responsible therapist, I recognise that eating disorders and complex health needs require medical supervision alongside therapy, and there may be instances where therapy is not safe or appropriate. Where needed, I work collaboratively with your GP, psychiatrist, or HCPC-registered dietitian.
